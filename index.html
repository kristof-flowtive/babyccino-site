Our story

A dream that grew with our family.

Babyccino was a dream that flourished as we expanded our family. With just one month between getting the keys for renovations to start and our first baby boy being born, there was plenty to learn all round.

With a relative suggesting we were having a baby and a coffee shop, a Babyccino, the name stuck. We opened our doors, and truly our hearts, to the lovely community of Paddock Wood and beyond in January 2024.

Picked with care

Spice expertise, tried and tested recipes

With a background in hospitality from running our own curry house, it felt only right to bring that culinary knowledge to our newest venture. It means we can offer something different, bringing spice expertise along with tried and tested recipes.

We developed our menu to give a modern fusion of what you typically expect from a coffee shop, elevated with an Asian twist. You’ll find a real mix of dishes, so your perfect breakfast, brunch or lunch isn’t far away.
